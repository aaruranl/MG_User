How to Submit Your Request

Subject / Title of the Issue

Provide a clear and short title describing your problem.

Examples:
  • "Unable to login to my account"
  • "Subscription payment failed"
  • "Profile not loading"
  • "Report a fake profile"

Detailed Description

Explain the issue clearly. Please include:

What you were trying to do
What happened
Any error messages
When the issue started
Steps you've already tried to fix it (if any)

Evidence (Optional, but helpful)

If you have any proof, attach it to help us resolve the issue faster:

Screenshots
Screen recordings
Error codes
Payment receipts

Response Time

Our team will review your request and respond within:

24 to 48 hours

(excluding weekends or public holidays)

If the issue is complex, we may require additional time, but we will update you accordingly.

Important Notes
Security: Do NOT share your password or sensitive financial information.
Email Verification: Support can only respond to issues sent from the email linked to your MarriageGate account.
Conduct Policy: Abusive or spam messages may result in account restriction.
Emergency: For emergency safety concerns, please report immediately through the in-app Report Button and then email support.
